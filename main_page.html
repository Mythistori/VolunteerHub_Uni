How it will work

  1. Browse events and read their details.
  2. Choose to join as a participant or apply as a volunteer.
  3. For volunteering, select a role and submit an applicantion.
  4. Check your registration or application status in My Activity.

Organizers will be able to publish events, view participant lists, and accept or reject volunteer application.
